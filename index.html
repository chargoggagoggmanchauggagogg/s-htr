      Lock out other apps

      Bounce back (free): Infinity cannot see other apps, but Shortcuts can send you back here. Open Shortcuts, tap Automation, then +, then App. Choose the distracting apps, pick Is Opened and Run Immediately, tap Next, then New Blank Automation. Add the Open App action and choose Infinity. While a focus session is running, opening those apps jumps straight to this locked page. Switch the automation off when you are done.
